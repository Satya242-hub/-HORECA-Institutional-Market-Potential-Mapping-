# <mark>-HORECA-Institutional-Market-Potential-Mapping-</mark>

## <mark>Objective</mark>

Identify and prioritize HORECA outlets based on estimated market potential to support territory planning and sales prioritization.

## <mark>Business Context</mark>

This project is designed around a Nestlé Professional Sales Officer role, focusing on HORECA market mapping, outlet prioritization, territory coverage, and sales potential estimation.

## <mark>Project Structure</mark>

Outlet_Master — 40 mapped HORECA outlets with outlet attributes, estimated customers/day, potential score, priority, and monthly potential.
Reference — Reference lists used for categorization and data validation.
Dashboard — Summary KPIs, territory analysis, and outlet-count visualization.

## <mark>Key Metrics</mark>

Total Outlets: 40
High-Potential Outlets: 12
Average Customers/Day: 204
Estimated Monthly Potential: ₹2.21 Cr

## <mark>Methodology</mark>

### <mark>Potential Score</mark>

Outlets are scored based on estimated outlet size and daily customer potential.

80–100: High Potential
60–79: Medium Potential
Below 60: Low Potential

### <mark>Monthly Potential</mark>

Estimated using:

Customers/Day × ₹100 × 30

## <mark>Key Business Use Cases</mark>

Identify high-potential HORECA accounts
Prioritize sales visits
Compare territory potential
Identify outlet segments with higher opportunity
Support distributor and secondary-sales-force planning


## <mark>Important Note</mark>

Customer volumes and monthly potential are illustrative estimates created for portfolio analysis and are not actual Nestlé or outlet sales data.
